# demo-log.md — your numbers

Appended at the end of every round's DEMO phase (committed as `round-N demo`).
These are *your* measurements — later rounds compare against them, not against the
course's example numbers.

Format per round (each demo.md says exactly what to record):

```
## round N — YYYY-MM-DD
- <metric>: <your value>
- note: <one line in your own words>
```
